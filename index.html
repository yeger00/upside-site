Black falls down. White falls up. One buddy's floor is the other one's ceiling.

Questions

Do we need two phones?

No. Both players share one phone, lying flat between you. White plays from the top edge and Black from the bottom edge.

Can one person play alone?

Not really: every level needs both buddies working together. It's made to be played by two people side by side.

What ages is it for?

About 6 and up. The first levels are gentle, and later ones are real puzzles that grown-ups enjoy too. It's best with a parent, a sibling or a friend.

Does it need the internet?

No. Once it's installed, the full game works offline.

Is my progress saved?

Yes, on your device. If you finish the free demo first, the full game starts fresh, and it's quick to catch up.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not yet. On an iPhone or iPad you can play the free demo in Safari.

Can we make our own levels?

Yes! Try the level editor in your browser. You can share a level you made with a link, and play it right away.
