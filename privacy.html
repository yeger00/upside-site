Privacy

UpDown Buddies doesn't collect any information about you or your children.

In the game

The game has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. It doesn't ask for your name, email or location, and it doesn't send anything over the internet.

Your progress (which levels you've finished) and your settings (sound and music) are saved only on your own device. Deleting the app, or clearing the browser's data for the demo, removes them.

On this website

This website and the free demo are hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may keep standard technical logs, such as IP addresses, to run and protect its service; see GitHub's privacy statement. The website itself sets no cookies.

Buying the game

The full game is sold on itch.io, which handles the payment and the download. What itch.io collects is covered by its privacy policy.

Questions

Contact the developer through the game's itch.io page.
